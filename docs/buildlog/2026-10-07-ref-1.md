## Scope

REF-1 chooses an evidence-backed starting point for the thermal near-field core. It does **not**
claim physical validation and it does not implement the production Plume solver.

This sprint will:

- pin the checked-in PLUMES distributions and external Python reference identities;
- establish canonical software-regression cases and normalized comparison outputs;
- compare the single-port near-field mechanisms that MODEL-1 needs first;
- map similarity-profile, multiport/merging and far-field divergences without prematurely
  adopting those layers;
- identify independent literature/experiment candidates for formulation checks and hold-back;
- recommend which code is adapted/reused, which references remain external oracles, and which
  behaviours are deliberately not inherited.

## Non-goals

- no changes under `References/`;
- no calibration of a Plume model;
- no invented pass/fail tolerance;
- no claim that PLUMES/Visual Plumes agreement is physical validation or permitting proof;
- no multiport or far-field product implementation;

## Preliminary findings

### PLUMES shipped-example pair

The two checked-in distributions ship the same example CSV inputs. Their example `.prj` files
differ in two far-field option flags.

The near-field table in the two shipped `ModelResults_TxtOutputs.dat` files is identical through
the plume-surface event at step 275. The visible divergence begins in the far-field block:

- the SSMC-style example selects the 4/3-power eddy diffusivity and reports a 109.59 m wastefield
  width;
- the Dec-2025 example selects constant eddy diffusivity and reports 96.29 m.

Therefore this shipped pair is currently evidence of a **configuration difference in the far
field**, not evidence of a near-field version change. REF-1 must use controlled identical inputs
before drawing build-to-build conclusions.

### Ebb port

The pinned Ebb port is the leading direct-reuse/adaptation candidate because it is permissively
licensed and already separates near-field state/entrainment/merging/termination, ambient profiles,
cross-plume profiles, seawater properties and provenance.

For MODEL-1 the attractive seam is its single-port Lagrangian control-volume formulation:
mass, momentum, temperature and salinity are advanced as conserved quantities and the trajectory
is integrated independently of presentation.

Important caveat: the port intentionally carries both reference-parity behaviour and corrected or
alternative behaviour. REF-1 must identify which mechanisms Plume wants rather than inheriting
defaults wholesale.

### SFEI Visual Plumes

The pinned SFEI code is an independent UM3 implementation with stratified ambient handling,
single/multiport behaviour, timeseries support and Brooks far field. Its GPL-3.0 licence makes it
the preferred **external behavioural oracle** for this project unless a deliberate later licence
decision says otherwise.

Its UM3 entrainment implementation is especially useful as an independent code path against the
Ebb port's decoded/reimplemented equations.

### Similarity profile warning

Ebb's executable experiments show that the PLUMES GUI exposes three cross-plume similarity
profiles. The default is parabolic; the documented 3/2-power profile is a non-default option; and
the executable's named Gaussian is materially more peaked than the common literature
`exp(-2 r^2/b^2)` form.

REF-1 therefore records similarity profile as a **separate field-reconstruction/model choice**.
Executable default, manual profile and literature profile must not be conflated. Selection remains
owned by `FIELD-PROFILE-1` / FIELD-1 unless REF-1 obtains enough independent evidence to retire it.

### Physical evidence / hold-back track

Software references above are not a physical hold-back set.

REF-1 will separately assess public experimental/literature data suitable for thermal/round-jet
mechanism checks. Candidate families include momentum-dominated single-port jets (Fan 1967),
buoyancy-dominated jets in current (Lee & Cheung 1991), and published plume/jet entrainment
theory. Multi-plume coalescence evidence such as Cenedese & Linden (2014) is routed to OUTLET-2.

A dataset used to tune/select a closure cannot then be cited as independent hold-back evidence.
If raw numerical data cannot be obtained with clear provenance, it remains literature evidence,
not a fabricated digitized golden case.

## REF-1B evidence — single-port mechanism bakeoff

Pinned Ebb/SFEI source and mechanism evidence is recorded in
`tests/reference/reference-lock.json`; the full decision matrix is in
`docs/reference/REF1_NEARFIELD_BAKEOFF.md`.

Major findings:

- Ebb's MIT single-port Lagrangian-control-volume structure is the preferred adaptation source.
- SFEI's GPL UM3 implementation is an independent oracle/source witness, not product code.
- Ebb and SFEI independently support the source contraction relation and element
  `h proportional to |V|` stretching used to derive radius.
- The cross-current closure is not safely reconstructed as a naive
  "Taylor entrainment + every published projected-area term" sum. SFEI source independently
  corroborates Ebb's decoded coupling/cancellation inside the UM3 aspiration/cylinder treatment.
- Ebb's current-sweep validation therefore justifies carrying a decoded **UM3-reference closure**
  for regression, but executable parity does not prove that every legacy behavior is the best
  physical closure.
- In particular, the legacy single-plume curvature contribution is effectively inert in the SFEI
  implementation and improves PLUMES parity when omitted, while published PAE theory assigns a
  physical curvature contribution. This disagreement is routed to `MODEL-CLOSURE-1`.
- Plume production thermodynamics should use **TEOS-10**, not PLUMES' old Knudsen density relation
  and not Ebb's EOS-80 science default.
- Product coordinates are fixed as ENU; reference-axis conventions are adapter concerns.

## REF-1C evidence — physical triangulation

Independent evidence is recorded in `docs/reference/REF1_LITERATURE.md`.

The qualification split is:

- **Fan (1967)**: formulation/calibration family.
- **Lee & Cheung (1991)**: untouched heated, buoyancy-dominated current hold-back.
- **Seckin et al. (2026)**: independent secondary evidence that UM3 agrees more strongly with the
  mostly momentum-dominated Fan data than the Lee-Cheung buoyancy-dominated data.

REF-1 deliberately does not digitize plotted observations merely to create numerical goldens.
Machine-readable/tabulated experiment rows with clear provenance are a MODEL-1 qualification
deliverable.

The final production current-entrainment closure and tolerances are therefore **not hidden REF-1
assumptions**. They are explicit gates:

- `MODEL-CLOSURE-1`: select/freeze the physical closure using Fan, then open Lee-Cheung hold-back
  without retuning.
- `MODEL-TOL-1`: derive thresholds from the resulting calibration/hold-back error distributions.
