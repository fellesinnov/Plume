## Source candidate

- `src/plume/field/reconstruction.py` implements a 3-D finite curved-tube field reconstruction, using MODEL-1 transport states and the injected thermodynamics boundary. A 3/2-power bounded cross-sectional profile is mapped from the actual centerline/radius and local ambient.
- `src/plume/field/__init__.py` exports headless slice/point APIs.
- `src/plume/render/plots.py` renders ambient temperature and local in-situ `delta_T` on separate labelled scales, with physical section/depth-sliced plan axes and a model-computed 2 deg C contour.
- `src/plume/render/__init__.py` and the optional `pyproject.toml` `plot` extra keep Matplotlib out of the headless physics runtime dependency list.
- `tests/field/test_field1.py` contains focused numerical and adversarial regression checks.
- `docs/reference/FIELD1_PROFILE.md` owns the EPA reference, mathematics, provisional source-limited profile transition, reconstruction/coordinate contract and physical debt.

The cross-section `35/9` limiting factor is backed by EPA/600/B-24/339, section 3.3.1. The near-source interpolation is **not** provided as a physically qualified law by the report; it is an explicit protective engineering assumption with independent field-profile evidence owed. The 3-D nearest-segment tube also makes its own self-overlap and curvature approximation explicit.
